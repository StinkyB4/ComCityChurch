Missional Communities

The Church Scattered

Missional Communities scatter into the everyday rhythms of life to encourage, bless, serve, and care for one another, our neighbours, and our city.

What Is a Missional Community?

A Family of Servant Missionaries

A Missional Community (MC) is a Family of Missionary Servants sent as Disciples who make Disciples in the everyday stuff of life. A Missional Community is a gospel-centered community with an intentional mission. Our MCs meet together regularly to eat, study God's Word, encourage one another, and press on to Godly love and good works.

We consider each missional community to be an intentional group of Christ-followers in a particular area of Winnipeg, reaching their own neighbours together. They know their context best, and as a community, they align themselves with the rhythms of life in their neighbourhood.
