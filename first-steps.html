First Steps

You don't have to have it all figured out. Start with one step.

Start Here

Everybody Starts Somewhere

Maybe you've been walking with Jesus for decades. Maybe you haven't set foot in a church since you were a kid. Maybe you're not sure you believe any of it and you're just curious. Wherever you're coming from, you're welcome here — and there's a next step for you.

These four steps aren't a program to complete or a hoop to jump through. They're simply the path most people take as they move from visiting to belonging. Take them in order, or take the one that fits where you are right now.

The Path

Four Steps

Simple, unhurried, and always at your own pace.

1

Come and See

The first step is the easiest one: show up. Join us on a Sunday — coffee at 10:00am, worship at 10:30am. Come as you are, sit wherever you like, and leave whenever you need to. There's no pressure and nobody will single you out.

Plan Your Visit →
2

Hear the Good News

Everything we do rests on one message: that God has come to rescue us in Jesus Christ. Before anything else, it's worth understanding what that message actually is — and what it would mean for you to trust it.

Read the Gospel →
3

Find Your People

Sunday is where we gather; our Missional Communities are where we're known. They meet through the week in homes across Winnipeg for food, honest conversation, prayer, and life on mission together. This is where visiting turns into belonging.

Explore Communities →
4

Follow Jesus Together

Following Jesus was never meant to be a solo project. Baptism, membership, serving on a team, being discipled and discipling others — these are the ordinary ways we grow. Tell us where you are and we'll help you take the next step.
