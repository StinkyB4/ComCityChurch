The Church Gathered

The Church Gathered

Sunday Gatherings are a chance to gather our Missional Communities together as the whole church to celebrate the gospel of Jesus. We sing, encourage one another, pray, share stories of God's goodness, laugh, sometimes cry, submit to Scripture, participate in communion, and leave exhorted to be who we are as God's people — disciples of Jesus, a family of servants on his mission.

What to Expect

Come as You Are

No dress code, no performance required. Just come. You are welcome exactly as you are.

Sung Worship

We sing together to celebrate what God has done — declaring his worth through music and voice.

Scripture Preached

The Bible is opened and taught with care. We preach through books of the Bible, letting Scripture set the agenda.

Communion

We regularly celebrate the Lord's Supper together as a church — remembering Christ's death and proclaiming his return.

We'd Love to See You This Sunday

Let us know you're coming and we'll make sure someone is ready to welcome you.
