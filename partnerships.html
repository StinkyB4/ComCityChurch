Together for the Gospel

Our Partnerships

We believe the mission of Christ is bigger than any one church. We are grateful to pursue it alongside like-minded partners who share our convictions and our vision for the city and the world.

Why Partnerships Matter

Stronger Together

No local church can do it all alone. We are committed to partnering with other churches, networks, and organisations who are faithful to the gospel and serious about the mission. These partnerships strengthen our own community, hold us accountable to our convictions, and multiply our reach beyond what we could accomplish on our own.

We are grateful for every relationship that sharpens us, challenges us, and sends us back to our neighbourhood and our city with greater clarity and courage.
