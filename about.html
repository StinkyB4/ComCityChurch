A Church on Mission

A Church on Mission

We are a disciple-making community sent into Winnipeg. A disciple is anyone who follows Jesus as the Christ, is growing to submit every area of life to His presence and Lordship by the power of the Holy Spirit, and is beginning to take spiritual responsibility for others in their local church.

Our deep desire is to grow as disciples of Jesus in our love and worship of God, our love for each other, and our love for those who do not yet know the hope of the gospel.
