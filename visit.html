No Surprises

What to Expect on Sunday

Walking into a new church can feel a little nerve-wracking. Here's a walk-through of a typical Sunday morning — so you know exactly what you're stepping into.

1

9:50am — Doors Open

Find a Seat

The doors open a few minutes early. Come in, find a comfortable spot, and take a breath. Our space is casual — jeans are fine, suits are fine, come as you are.

2

10:00am — Coffee & Connection

Grab a Coffee

We serve free coffee and take the first 30 minutes to connect. This is intentional — we believe Sunday starts before the music does. Introduce yourself, meet a few people, settle the kids.

3

10:30am — The Gathering

Worship & the Word

We sing together, pray, and hear from God's Word through expository preaching. The service runs about 90 minutes. No fog machines, no performance — just gathered people encountering the risen Jesus.

4

~Noon — Stick Around

More Coffee. Good Conversation.

Some of the best moments happen after the service. No agenda — just people lingering, asking questions, and genuinely enjoying each other. We'd love to meet you in person.

Families Welcome

Bring the Whole Family.

We love having kids in the room with us. Children of all ages are welcome in the service — we'll have colouring sheets and quiet activities available for little ones. You're never a distraction. Come as you are, bring who you love.

How We Do Life

Gather. Grow. Go.

Three rhythms shape everything we do as a church. These aren't programs — they're a way of life for a people sent on mission.

01

Gather

Every Sunday we gather to worship, hear God's Word preached, and celebrate what He's doing among us. The Sunday gathering isn't the only thing we do — but it's the heartbeat that everything else flows from.

02

Grow

Life change happens in community. We grow as disciples through Missional Communities — small groups scattered across Winnipeg that share life, open their homes, and serve their neighbours together.

03

Go

We are a sent people. Every member is a missionary — to their neighbourhood, their workplace, and their city. We don't invite people into our programs; we go out to where they already are.
