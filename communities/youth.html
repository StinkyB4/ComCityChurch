Youth

Raising young people to live boldly for Jesus.

Who We Are

A Community for the Next Generation

The Youth Missional Community exists to raise up the next generation of disciples — young people who are passionate about Jesus, growing in love for each other, and urgent to see their friends know the hope of the gospel. We gather to study Scripture, worship, pray, and build the kind of friendships that go deep.

We don't believe in a faith that stays inside the church walls. We want young people to know who they are in Christ, to love their school and their city, and to live out the gospel in the everyday stuff of life — with their friends, their families, and their neighbourhoods. If your teen is looking for a community where they are known and where faith is taken seriously, we'd love for them to join us.

What to Expect

Life Together as Young Disciples

Real Community

Friendships that go beyond Sunday. We do life together — food, fun, and real conversation about real things.

Grounded in the Word

We take Scripture seriously. We want young people who know what they believe and why — and who are shaped by it.

On Mission

We encourage youth to see their school and friendships as their mission field — and to live accordingly.
