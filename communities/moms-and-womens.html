Women's MC

Growing Together in Every Season of Life

Who We Are

A Community of Women Growing in Faith Together

The Moms & Women's Missional Community is a place for women to find genuine friendship, encouragement, and gospel-rooted discipleship in the everyday rhythms of life. Whether you're a mom with young kids at home or a woman who wants to invest in the lives of others, there is a place for you here.

We believe women grow best together — through honest conversation, shared struggles, prayer, and time in God's Word. This is a community that wants to love one another well and see women of all stages of life walking faithfully with Jesus.

What to Expect

Life Together for Women

Life Together

A welcoming space for women to build real friendship — sharing meals, conversation, and the joys and challenges of everyday life.

Women Discipling Women

Older and younger women growing in faith together, encouraging one another in the gospel and in godly, everyday faithfulness.

Open to All Women

Whether you have young kids at home or simply want to pour into other women's lives, this community has a place for you.
