Who We Are

A Community of Disciples in River Heights

The River Heights Missional Community is a gospel-centered family of disciples living and serving in the River Heights neighbourhood of Winnipeg. We gather to eat together, study God's Word, pray, and encourage one another. And we scatter into the rhythms of our neighbourhood — knowing our neighbours, serving the people around us, and sharing the hope we have in Jesus.

We are not a program — we are a community on mission together. If you live in or near River Heights and are looking for a place to belong, we'd be glad to have you join us.

What to Expect

Life Together in the Neighbourhood

Shared Meals

We eat together regularly. Food is one of the simplest ways to build real friendship.

Word & Prayer

We study Scripture together and pray for our neighbours, our city, and one another.

Neighbourhood Mission

We look for ways to bless and serve the people around us — not as a program, but as a way of life.
