Women's MC

Women's Prayer Call

United in Prayer. Every Monday Morning.

Who We Are

Seeking the Lord Together, Every Week

The Women's Prayer Call is a weekly Google Meet gathering for women of the church to come together and pray. Every Monday morning, women from across the city join a call to bring their requests, burdens, and praise before the Lord — together.

All women are welcome. Whether you've been praying for years or are still finding your footing, this call is a simple and accessible way to stay connected to other women and to God throughout the week.

What to Expect

A Simple, Faithful Rhythm of Prayer

Weekly Prayer

Every Monday morning we bring our requests, burdens, and praise before the Lord together — believing God hears and answers.

Join From Anywhere

All you need is a phone or computer. The Google Meet format makes it easy to join from home, work, or wherever your Monday morning finds you.

Open to All Women

Whether you've been praying for years or are just learning how, every woman is welcome on this call.

When We Meet

Every Monday Morning

Day Every Monday

Format Google Meet (Virtual)

Email us to receive the Google Meet link before your first call.
