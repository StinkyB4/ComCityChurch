Saturate Winnipeg

A day for pastors and leaders to pray, learn, and lean in around disciple-making in our city — together.

Register Now → Event Details
The Invitation

What would it look like for the good news of Jesus to saturate Winnipeg?

Spend a day with pastors and leaders praying and leaning in around disciple-making in our city together.

What if disciple-making is not one more program you need to launch, but equipping and releasing everyday disciples to live as His people where they live, work, learn, and play? And what if you don't need to figure out what that looks like on your own, but within the context of a community of other leaders, working in the city together?

We believe God is already at work in Winnipeg. This is an invitation to gather and together prayerfully consider how we may join Him in that work. We hope you'll join us.

Who It's For

Pastors, leaders, and everyday disciple-makers

This day is shaped for pastors, church planters, ministry leaders, and anyone helping others follow Jesus in the everyday places God has already sent them. Bring your co-leaders. Bring a pastor friend. Come hungry for the gospel and ready to see God saturate our city.

Featured Speaker

Jeff Vanderstelt

Executive Director, Saturate · Founder, Soma

Jeff serves as the Executive Director of Saturate. He is also the Founder of Soma, a global family of churches committed to seeing every woman, man and child meet Jesus through His body on mission everyday. He regularly works with teams toward gospel saturation in greater Seattle, Charleston, and Tokyo. Jeff has authored Saturate, Gospel Fluency, and Making Space.

He lives in Seattle, WA with his wife Jayne and their three children.
