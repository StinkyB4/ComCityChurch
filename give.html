Generosity

Give

Generous giving is an act of worship.

Support the Mission

Support the Mission


As a church family on mission, we depend on the generous giving of our members to sustain ministry, support our pastors and staff, and extend the gospel into Winnipeg and beyond. Giving is an act of worship — a declaration that our trust is in God, not money.

Commissioned City Church is a registered charitable organization in Canada, legally permitted to issue tax-deductible receipts for all donations.
