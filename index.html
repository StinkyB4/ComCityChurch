Welcome to Commissioned City Church


We are a church in Winnipeg committed to making disciples who make disciples. We believe the gospel changes everything — how we live, love, and serve our city.

Whether you're exploring faith or have followed Jesus for years, there's a place for you here.

Gather

Every Sunday we gather as the whole church to celebrate the gospel through worship, Scripture, and community.

Grow

We grow as disciples through Missional Communities, Scripture, and intentional life-on-life relationships.

Go

We scatter into our neighbourhoods as missionaries, sent to bless and serve our city with the hope of Jesus.

The Best News

The Gospel Changes Everything

God, in his love, entered our world in Jesus Christ — living the life we couldn't, dying the death we deserved, and rising victorious, promising us hope! This is news that transforms how we live, who we love, and why we exist.
